Common Plant Diseases

Learn about common plant diseases and their symptoms.

🍂

Early Blight

Common in tomato and potato plants. Causes brown spots and yellowing of leaves.

🌫️

Powdery Mildew

Appears as white powder-like patches on leaves and stems.

🍃

Leaf Rust

Produces orange or reddish-brown spots on affected leaves.

🌱

Leaf Mosaic

Causes uneven green and yellow patterns and may affect plant growth.

How the system works

Step 1: Upload a clear plant leaf image.

Step 2: A trained machine-learning model analyzes the image.

Step 3: The system displays the predicted disease and possible treatment guidance.

Plant care tip: Check leaves regularly, avoid excessive watering, and remove infected plant material when appropriate.
